EMPIRICAL GOTCHA (appended to issues.md)
  `git ls-tree -r --long` separates the size with SPACE PADDING (right-aligned),
  then a TAB before the path: `100644 blob <sha>       3\ta.txt`. A regex
  assuming `<sha>\t<size>\t<path>` silently parses ZERO entries — the tree leg
  and all content rules vanish while history-leg findings still look plausible.
  Matcher: /^(\d{6}) (\w+) ([0-9a-f]{40})\s+(\d+)\t(.+)$/ + type=="blob" filter.
